# WhiteGold Business Plan

## Executive Summary

WhiteGold is a premium farm-to-home dairy brand focused on delivering fresh and healthy dairy products directly to customers through a subscription-based and on-demand ordering platform.

The company aims to eliminate middlemen, improve product quality, ensure transparency, and create a trusted relationship between dairy farms and consumers.

WhiteGold combines dairy farming, technology, delivery management, and customer subscriptions into a single ecosystem.

## Vision

To become India's most trusted premium dairy brand powered by technology and farm transparency.

## Mission

To deliver fresh, hygienic, high-quality dairy products directly from farms to customers while ensuring convenience, affordability, and customer satisfaction.
