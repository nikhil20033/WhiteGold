# WhiteGold Go-To-Market Strategy

## Overview

The go-to-market strategy defines how WhiteGold will acquire customers, build brand awareness, and establish a sustainable dairy delivery business.

---

## Launch City

Phase 1 Launch Area:
- Pune
- Haveli
- Nearby Residential Societies

Target Radius:
- 10 to 15 KM from processing center

---

## Initial Customer Target

Month 1:
- 25 Customers

Month 3:
- 100 Customers

Month 6:
- 300 Customers

Month 12:
- 1000 Customers
