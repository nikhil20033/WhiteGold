# WhiteGold Risk Analysis

## Overview

This document identifies potential business, operational, financial, and technical risks associated with the WhiteGold platform and outlines mitigation strategies.

---

## Business Risks

### Low Customer Adoption

Risk:
Customers may be slow to adopt a new dairy brand.

Impact:
High

Probability:
Medium

Mitigation:
- Free product sampling
- Introductory offers
- Referral programs
- Society-level marketing campaigns
