# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Version: 1.0

Project Name: White Gold
 
---

# 1. Business Overview

White Gold is a premium Direct-to-Customer (D2C) dairy platform that delivers fresh milk and dairy products directly from farms to customers.

The platform aims to remove middlemen, ensure product quality, and provide a seamless subscription-based experience.

---

# 2. Vision

To become India's most trusted premium dairy brand powered by technology.

---

# 3. Mission

Deliver fresh, hygienic, farm-fresh dairy products directly to customers while providing transparency, convenience, and exceptional service.

---

# 4. Business Goals

- Build a trusted premium dairy brand
- Deliver fresh milk every morning
- Subscription-based recurring revenue
- Reduce operational costs using technology
- Expand across Pune and Maharashtra

---

# 5. Target Customers

- Families
- Working professionals
- Fitness enthusiasts
- Parents with children
- Health-conscious consumers

---

# 6. Products

- A2 Milk
- Cow Milk
- Buffalo Milk
- Ghee
- Paneer
- Curd
- Butter
- Buttermilk

---

# 7. Revenue Model

- Daily subscriptions
- One-time purchases
- Monthly subscription plans
- Premium products
