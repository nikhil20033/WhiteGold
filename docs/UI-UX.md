# UI/UX Design

Version: 1.0

Project Name: White Gold

---

# 1. Overview

The White Gold platform is designed with a clean, modern, and user-friendly interface to provide an excellent experience for customers, administrators, delivery partners, and farm managers.

---

# 2. Design Principles

- Simple and minimal UI
- Mobile-first design
- Fast navigation
- Responsive layouts
- Accessible interface
- Consistent color palette

---

# 3. User Roles

## Customer

- Browse Products
- Subscribe to Milk Plans
- Place Orders
- Track Deliveries
- Make Payments
- Manage Profile

---

## Admin

- Dashboard
- Product Management
- Customer Management
- Order Management
- Subscription Management
- Reports & Analytics

---

## Delivery Partner

- Today's Deliveries
- Delivery Status
- Route Information
- Customer Details

---

## Farm Manager

- Milk Production
- Inventory
- Quality Reports
- Dispatch Management
