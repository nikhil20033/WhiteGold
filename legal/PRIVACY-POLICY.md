# WhiteGold Privacy Policy

## Information Collection

WhiteGold collects:

- Customer Name
- Email Address
- Mobile Number
- Delivery Address
- Order Information
- Payment Information

## Usage

Customer information is used for:

- Order Processing
- Subscription Management
- Delivery Operations
- Customer Support
- Platform Improvements

## Data Security

WhiteGold implements industry-standard security practices to protect customer information.

## Data Sharing

Customer information will not be sold to third parties.

Information may be shared only with:

- Delivery Partners
- Payment Providers
- Legal Authorities when required

## Customer Rights

Customers may:

- View their data
- Request corrections
- Request account deletion

## Contact

Privacy-related requests can be submitted to WhiteGold customer support.
