# WhiteGold Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Refunds may be issued for:

- Incorrect product delivery
- Damaged products
- Missing items

## Non-Eligible Cases

Refunds will not be issued for:

- Product consumption
- Incorrect customer information
- Customer unavailability

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed within 5 to 7 business days.

## Contact

Customers may contact support for refund-related requests.
