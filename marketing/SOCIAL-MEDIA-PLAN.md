# WhiteGold Social Media Plan

## Objective

Build brand awareness, generate leads, and convert customers into subscribers.

## Platforms

- Instagram
- Facebook
- WhatsApp Business
- YouTube

## Content Categories

- Farm Fresh Milk Content
- Dairy Product Education
- Customer Testimonials
- Behind The Scenes
- Farm Operations
- Product Promotions

## Posting Frequency

Instagram: Daily
Facebook: Daily
WhatsApp Status: Daily
YouTube: Weekly

## Monthly Goals

- Increase brand awareness
- Generate leads
- Increase subscriptions
- Improve customer engagement

## KPI Tracking

- Followers
- Reach
- Engagement Rate
- Leads Generated
- Subscription Conversions
